## New repeated demand roots

### sourdough starter

King Arthur Baking and Breadtopia both maintain dedicated starter creation, feeding, readiness, and maintenance resources. The root expands naturally into `sourdough starter recipe`, `how to feed sourdough starter`, `sourdough starter maintenance`, and related queries.

### starter feeding ratio

King Arthur Baking explicitly tests multiple sourdough feeding ratios, while Breadtopia and The Perfect Loaf use feeding ratio / hydration / inoculation as recurring starter-management concepts. This forms a distinct calculation/understanding demand family.

### bulk fermentation

King Arthur Baking, Breadtopia, The Perfect Loaf ecosystem pages, and dedicated sourdough tools repeatedly treat bulk fermentation as a distinct stage with timing, temperature, rise, and troubleshooting subqueries.

### bulk fermentation calculator

Multiple independent sites provide dedicated calculators estimating sourdough bulk fermentation from temperature, starter percentage, hydration, flour type, or target rise. The repeated tool form is directly observable in SERPs.

### fermentation time calculator

Independent tools exist for sourdough-specific and broader fermentation timing, including sourdough, bread, kombucha, yogurt, sauerkraut, and other ferments. The root is broader than bulk fermentation alone.

### fermentation brine calculator

Multiple independent tools calculate salt percentage / brine quantities for lacto-fermented vegetables, while Cultures For Health separately documents salt/brine as a core repeated fermentation problem.

### fermentation troubleshooting

Cultures For Health maintains a dedicated troubleshooting FAQ covering mold, kahm yeast, brine level, slime, odor, color, and stalled fermentation. King Arthur Baking similarly maintains recurring sourdough starter problem/diagnostic guidance. This supports a reusable diagnostic information root.

### sourdough schedule

King Arthur Baking has dedicated sourdough feeding/baking schedule guidance, and fermentation calculator sites expose timeline/schedule planning as a recurring workflow. This is a planning demand family distinct from a single proofing-time query.
